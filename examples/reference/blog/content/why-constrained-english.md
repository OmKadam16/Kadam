Every sentence in Kadam matches **exactly one** template, so a page either
compiles or tells you which line is wrong and why.

## What that buys you

- No guessing what a line means: `it is centered` always centers the box.
- Mistakes come with suggestions, like *did you mean: year?*
- The output is plain HTML and CSS you can read.

Read the [language spec](https://github.com/OmKadam16/Kadam) for the full grammar.
